About us

Three decades of honest packaging.

Our story

From a single Nairobi line to East Africa's packaging partner.

Established in 1995, King Plastic Industries Limited began converting paper for Nairobi's grocers and millers. Three decades on, we run a vertically integrated plant — printing, lamination, slitting and bag-making — supplying everything from block-bottom grocery bags to 50 kg multi-wall industrial sacks.

We build packaging the honest way: locally made, to international standard, for brands that carry the weight of everyday Kenya.

Our Mission

To manufacture dependable, food-safe and sustainable packaging that helps East African businesses grow.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted converter — setting the standard for quality, service and environmental care.

Our Values

Integrity in every order, craftsmanship in every bag, and responsibility to the people and place we call home.
